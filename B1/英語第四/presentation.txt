Introduction
Hello everyone. Today, we are going to talk about how AI is changing university students’ daily life.
AI has become very common in our lives, especially for students.

How Students Use AI
Many students use AI tools for studying.
For example, AI can explain difficult topics, summarize long texts, and help with English writing.
Some students also use AI to search for information faster or manage their schedules.
Now, I will talk about the benefits of AI for university students.
One major benefit is efficiency.
Students can save time by using AI to understand difficult subjects more quickly.
Another benefit is support.
AI can help students who are not confident in English or academic writing.
Because of AI, learning can become easier and more accessible for many students.
However, AI also has some problems.
If students depend too much on AI, they may stop thinking by themselves.
This can reduce critical thinking skills.
Also, using AI improperly for reports can be considered cheating.

In our opinion, AI should be used as a support tool, not a replacement for thinking.
Students should understand the content by themselves.

In conclusion, AI is changing university students’ daily life in many ways.
If we use AI wisely, it can be a powerful tool for learning.
Thank you for listening.
